## Description

KELT-9b's dayside reaches about 4,600 kelvin. Even its nightside glows at roughly 2,600 K.
Models suggest hydrogen molecules split apart in the daytime and re-form at night, helping carry heat around this ultra-hot gas giant.

Visualization based on real data:
- Dayside / nightside brightness temperatures: 4566 +140/−136 K and 2556 +101/−97 K at 4.5 microns.
- Orbital period: 1.4811235 days, or about 36 hours.
- Planet radius: 1.936 Jupiter radii; semi-major axis: 0.03368 AU.
- Stellar radius: 2.418 solar radii. The orbit is nearly polar relative to the star.
