On November 18, 2026, Voyager 1 becomes the first machine ever a full light-day from Earth.
From out there the Sun is just the brightest star — right below Orion — and a radio signal from Earth needs 24 hours to arrive.

Visualization based on real data:
• One light-day = 25,902,068,371 km (173.14 AU). NASA: Voyager 1 reaches it from Earth on Nov 18, 2026, 10:16 UTC
• Voyager 1 trajectory (1977–2026) and planet positions: NASA/JPL Horizons
• From Voyager 1 the Sun is a magnitude −15.6 star, about 4° from Rigel in Orion (computed from Horizons)
• Light-travel times from Earth on Nov 18, 2026: Sun 8 min, Jupiter 43 min, Saturn 1 h 13 min, Neptune 4 h 04 min (computed from Horizons)
