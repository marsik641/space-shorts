## Description

Enceladus hides a global ocean under an icy crust. Near its south pole, fractures release
water vapor and ice grains, carrying chemical clues from the ocean into space.
Cassini data revealed phosphate salts in ice grains. Life has not been found there.

Visualization based on real data:
- Enceladus is about 504 km across.
- A global ocean is inferred from Cassini measurements of its libration.
- Phosphate salts were identified in salt-rich ice grains collected in Saturn's E ring.
